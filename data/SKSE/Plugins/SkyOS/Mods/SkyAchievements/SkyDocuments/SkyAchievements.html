ACHIEVEMENTS & DISCOVERIES

SkyAchievements

Unlock named achievements and review save-specific counters, quest completions, and paged item and location discoveries. SkyRewrite and SkyCollection supply optional action and discovery records.

Achievements and records

SkyAchievements contains Achievements, Lifetime Records, Quests Completed, Locations Discovered, and Items Discovered. SkyCollection supplies optional discovery records. SkyRewrite supplies optional adventure-history data.

Unlock message

When an achievement unlocks, SkyAchievements can show a dedicated in-game notice with its icon, name, and requirement. Use SkyViewer’s Achievement unlock message module settings to turn it on or off and choose its design, size, and screen position.

Requirement data

Player level and faction membership come from the active Skyrim character. SkyRewrite supplies completed quests, cumulative creature and stolen-septim counters, gold progress, verified brawl outcomes, and recent named actions. Some named-creature requirements rely on retained history and may not include events recorded before SkyRewrite was installed. SkyCollection supplies item and location discoveries; Tomb Raider uses the discovered-location total as its dungeon-clears count.

Gold achievements: Golden Touch and Legend Of The Hoard use SkyRewrite's accurate gold tracking, including pickups, looting, and transfers. Progress records the character's peak gold balance, so later purchases do not erase an achievement already earned. Brawl Champion counts confirmed player wins from SkyRewrite brawl records; losses do not count.

Lifetime records also expose detailed hostile NPC and monster groups, theft categories, Thieves Guild jobs, quest totals, ingredients, books, spells, and shouts. Discovery lists begin with 50 records and load more only when requested.

Other character features: The Dashboard, Factions, Properties, Name Cards, missions, SkyPoints, SkyStore, player-card artwork, and mission-completion notice are owned by the separate SkyMissions app. SkyRewrite and SkyCollection remain optional integrations.

Requirements

  • Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition on a supported runtime.
  • SKSE, PrismaUI, and SkyOS.
  • SkyRewrite and SkyCollection provide optional achievement data; achievements that need an unavailable source wait for it.
